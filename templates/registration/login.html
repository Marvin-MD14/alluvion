About Project Alluvion

Integrating Artificial Intelligence, Remote Sensing, and Geospatial Analysis to build a safer, more resilient community.

SAR Satellite Data

Processes Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery to detect flooded areas, even through clouds and at night.

AI Detection

Uses Otsu thresholding and machine learning to automatically classify water vs. land pixels with high accuracy.

Weather Integration

Correlates PAGASA rainfall data with flood events to predict the time and magnitude of flooding.

How It Works

A streamlined pipeline from satellite image to actionable flood map.

1

Upload SAR

Upload Sentinel-1 SAR images acquired near the flood event date.

2

AI Analysis

The system applies Otsu thresholding to detect water and calculate flood extent.

3

Generate Map

Creates interactive hazard maps showing Low, Moderate, and High flood risk zones.

4

Evaluate & Report

Compare predictions with ground truth and export detailed PDF reports.
